# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in TeleZip a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Welcome:** empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing
opinions, and focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**Unacceptable:** sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insults,
harassment (public or private), publishing others' private information
(including credentials or chat IDs), and any conduct that would
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report violations privately via the Security tab's private vulnerability
reporting or by contacting the maintainers through their GitHub
profiles. All reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, issues, and
comments that violate this Code, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors for serious or repeated violations. This Code applies in all
project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions, releases).
